## 1. Overview

### Business Model
Costco Wholesale Corporation operates membership-based warehouse clubs and e-commerce sites in the United States, Canada, Puerto Rico, Mexico, Japan, the United Kingdom, Korea, Australia, Taiwan, China, Spain, France, Sweden, Iceland and New Zealand, with 939 warehouses worldwide as of August 30 2026 [10-K L162: "Costco Wholesale Corporation and its subsidiaries (Costco or the Company) began operations in 1983, in Seattle, Washington. We are principally engaged in the operation of membership warehouses ... Costco operated 939, 914, and 890 warehouses worldwide at August 30, 2026"].
The core concept is to offer low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products, driving high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover [10-K L168: "We operate membership warehouses and e-commerce sites based on the concept that offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products ... will produce high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover"].
Merchandise is purchased directly from suppliers and routed to cross-docking depots or directly to warehouses, creating freight-volume efficiencies and lowering handling costs [10-K L170: "We buy most of our merchandise directly from suppliers and route it to cross-docking consolidation points (depots) or directly to our warehouses"].
Each warehouse averages about 148,000 sq ft and carries fewer than 4,000 active SKUs, substantially less than typical broadline retailers [10-K L182: "We carry less than 4,000 active stock keeping units (SKUs) per warehouse in our core warehouse business"].
Membership fees generate a steady revenue stream; the annual fee in the U.S. is $65 and total cardholders at year-end 2026 were 150.4 million [10-K L214: "Our annual fee for memberships is $65 in the U.S."], [10-K L224: "Total cardholders | 150,400 | 145,200 | 136,800"].

## 2. Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

### Competitive Position and Growth Drivers
Costco competes worldwide with a broad set of wholesalers and retailers-including supermarkets, supercenters, online retailers, gasoline stations, hard discounters, department and specialty stores-and cites Walmart, Target, Kroger and Amazon among its most significant U.S. competitors [10-K L278: "We compete on a worldwide basis with global, national, and regional wholesalers and retailers, ... Walmart, Target, Kroger, and Amazon are among our significant general merchandise retail competitors in the U.S."].
The company leverages its private-label brand Kirkland Signature, which it describes as high-quality, lower-priced than national brands, and a margin-enhancing differentiator [10-K L282: "We believe that Kirkland Signature products are high quality, offered at prices that are generally lower than national brands, and help lower costs, differentiate our merchandise offerings, and generally earn higher margins"].
Warehouse expansion remains a primary growth driver; Costco opened 28 new warehouses (including three relocations) in 2026, delivering 25 net-new locations (18 in the U.S., five in Canada, two internationally) [10-K L710: "We opened 28 new warehouses, including three relocations, for a total of 25 net new warehouses: 18 in the U.S., five in our Canadian segment, and two in our Other International segment"].
Ancillary businesses contribute materially, with gasoline stations numbering 774 at year-end 2026 and representing 11% of total net sales [10-K L200-L202: "We operated 774 gas stations at the end of 2026. Our gasoline business represented 11% of total net sales in 2026"].
Digital commerce is accelerating; digitally-enabled sales represented 11% of total net sales [10-K L206-L208: "Digitally-enabled sales, which represents sales that are initiated through a digital device, ... represented 11% of total net sales in 2026"] and digitally-enabled comparable sales grew 21% in 2026 [10-K L782: "Digitally-enabled comparable sales increased 21% during 2026"].
Strong membership renewal rates reinforce recurring fee revenue and traffic, with 92.3% renewal in the U.S. and Canada and 89.8% worldwide [10-K L232: "Our member renewal rate was 92.3% in the U.S. and Canada and 89.8% worldwide at the end of 2026"].
