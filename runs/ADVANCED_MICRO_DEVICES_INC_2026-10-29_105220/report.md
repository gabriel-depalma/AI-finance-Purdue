# ADVANCED MICRO DEVICES INC (AMD): equity research report

**Recommendation: Hold** · share price used: $578.44

## 1. Overview

### Business Model
AMD designs, manufactures and sells a broad portfolio of high-performance CPUs, GPUs, APUs, DPUs, AI NICs, FPGAs and adaptive SoCs that power data-center, client-PC and embedded applications, delivering end-to-end compute solutions. [10-K L162: "AMD drives innovation in high performance and AI computing to solve the world's most important challenges."] It also provides a comprehensive software stack-including development tools, compilers and drivers-to enable its silicon across these product families. [10-K L164: "We develop comprehensive software stacks that include development tools, compilers and drivers to enable our high-performance CPUs, APUs, GPUs and FPGAs."]

The company reports three primary reportable segments: the Data Center segment, which primarily includes AI accelerators, server CPUs, GPUs, APUs, DPUs and AI NICs; the Client and Gaming segment, which primarily includes CPUs, APUs, chipsets for desktops and notebooks, discrete GPUs and semi-custom SoC products; and the Embedded segment, which primarily includes embedded CPUs, APUs, FPGAs, system-on-modules and adaptive SoCs. [10-K L188: "the Data Center segment, which primarily includes AI accelerators, CPUs for servers, GPUs, APUs, DPUs, AI NICs,"] [10-K L190: "the Client and Gaming segment, which primarily includes CPUs, APUs, chipsets for desktops and notebooks, discrete GPUs, and semi-custom SoC products and development services;"] [10-K L192: "the Embedded segment, which primarily includes embedded CPUs, APUs, FPGAs, SOMs, and adaptive SoC products."]

AMD sells its products directly, through OEMs, ODMs and independent distributors, and also licenses portions of its IP portfolio. [10-K L284: "We sell our products via a direct sales force, our OEMs ... and through independent distributors and sales representatives."]

AI-centric positioning: the company believes AI will reshape computing and that its full potential will be realized when pervasive across cloud, edge and endpoint devices. [10-K L168: "We believe AI is shaping the next era of computing and its full potential will be realized when it becomes pervasive across cloud, edge and endpoint devices."] With its compute engines, IP, software enablement and deep expertise, AMD states it is positioned to lead this next computing era. [10-K L168: "With our compute engines, intellectual property, software enablement and deep expertise, AMD is positioned to lead in this next computing era."]

## 2. Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

AMD operates in markets that are "highly competitive and rapidly evolving." [10-K L360: "The markets in which our products are sold are highly competitive and rapidly evolving"] In the Data Center segment, AMD competes primarily against Intel and Nvidia.
